Bridging Financial Accuracy & Digital Intelligence

I am Md. Rashedun Nabi — a dynamic, results-oriented accounting and finance professional with a strong foundation in core accounting, cost & management accounting, and financial analysis. My career has been driven by a singular mission: transforming raw financial data into strategic intelligence that guides leadership decisions.

Currently serving as Officer — Costing & Budgeting at Ha-Meem Group, one of Bangladesh's largest apparel conglomerates, I manage pre-costing, post-costing, variance analysis, P&L tracking, and budget control across multiple product lines and buyers.

My unique edge lies at the intersection of traditional financial expertise and modern analytical tools — Power BI dashboards, Advanced Excel models, and AI-assisted analysis — enabling me to deliver insights faster and more accurately than ever before.
